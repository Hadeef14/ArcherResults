Run a competition in 3 steps

1

Create it

Click “New competition”, fill in the name, country, venue and dates. Takes less than a minute.

2

Enter scores

Add archers and record their arrow scores as the shooting happens — per end, per distance.

3

Publish results

Standings, brackets and rankings update automatically and can be shared with a single link.
